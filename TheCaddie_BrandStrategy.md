## THE PRODUCT PROMISE

This is the most important thing you write. It goes on the landing page, in every ad, in onboarding. It is why people download and why people pay.

### The 10-Round Promise

> Give me 10 rounds and I'll show you patterns about your mental game that even you didn't know existed.

Then deliver it. Here's the arc:

**Round 1:** The AI knows you from the Master Quiz. First message already feels personal.

**Round 3:** The 20-min check-in catches you mid-spiral on the back 9. You recover instead of collapse. You remember this.

**Round 5:** Mental Handicap moves from 31 → 47. You watch the number change on the chart. This is when it clicks.

**Round 7:** Pattern Intelligence fires. "Your most common trigger: closing holes when you're ahead." You've always felt this. Now you have proof.

**Round 10:** You shoot your lowest round in two years. The Caddie prepared you for it. You share the round card. Your friends ask what app you're using.

**This is the story you tell. This is the hook you sell.**

## SOCIAL CONTENT STRATEGY

### The Platforms

**TikTok** — Primary growth channel. GolfTok is massive and hungry for genuine mental game content. Younger audience (18-35), will share willingly, drives downloads fast.

**Instagram Reels** — Secondary. Same content, slightly more polished. Better for 25-45 demographic. Stories for round cards + mental score reveals.

**Snapchat** — Tertiary. Use for shareable round card integration (Snapchat has native app card sharing). More casual, Gen Z.

### Content Pillars

**1. "What my caddie said"** (most shareable)
Format: You're mid-round, having a moment. Cut to a screenshot of what the AI coach said. Brief reaction.
Hook: "I was about to blow up on 15. My caddie said this."
Why it works: Personal, relatable, shows the product working. Every golfer has had that hole.

**2. "Mental score reveal"** (weekly habit content)
Format: After each round, post your Mental Handicap score.
Hook: "Mental Handicap check — 72. ⬆️ up 6 from last week."
Why it works: Gameable. People will post their own. Creates a community metric.
Target behavior: Others download the app to get their own score.

**3. "I was going to triple bogey... then this happened"** (before/after)
Format: Describe a mental collapse moment. Show the coaching cue. Show the recovery.
Why it works: Golf is full of collapse stories. Recovery from collapse is aspirational.

**4. "The pattern check"** (analytical content, resonates with serious golfers)
Format: "After 8 rounds, the app figured out something I never knew about myself..."
Then reveal the pattern. Honest, specific, real.
Why it works: Data-driven golfers share this. The analytical community on Twitter/X amplifies it.

**5. "Cue word series"** (7-14 day challenge content)
Format: Pick one word (COMMIT / TRUST / RESET / BREATHE). Use it every round for 2 weeks. Track what happens.
Hook: "I used one word on every shot for 2 weeks. Here's what happened to my mental game."
Why it works: Challenge format performs consistently. Creates streaks of content.

**6. "The streak challenge"** (long-form series)
Format: "Using The Caddie for 30 rounds straight. I'm posting every single one."
Document the journey. Show wins. Show collapses. Show pattern discovery. Show the number moving.
Why it works: Ongoing content series that keeps people coming back. Authentic documentation > produced ads.

**7. "Things my caddie knows about my game that I didn't"** (vulnerability content)
Format: Share pattern insights, cue word history, mental handicap arc.
Why it works: Vulnerability in golf mental game content is rare and powerful. When someone shares "I found out I always choke under pressure on this specific situation" it resonates deeply.

### Posting rhythm
- TikTok: 4-5x/week (3 polished, 1-2 raw/real-time)
- Instagram: 3x/week Reels + daily Story (round card + daily focus)
- X/Twitter: Daily — the mental insight quote, data point, or provocation

### Founder-led vs. brand account

**Do both.** The founder account (you) drives trust and connection. The brand account drives distribution. The founder posts raw, real, personal. The brand curates and amplifies.

The founder account will grow faster. Get on camera. Show your own rounds. Your own mental collapses. Your own breakthroughs. That's the content that converts.

## BETA LAUNCH PLAN

### Who to invite

50-100 golfers. Not random. Curated. Look for:
- Active golfers who play 2+ rounds/month
- People who've talked about the mental game before (on social, in groups)
- Mix of handicaps (6-28 HCP is the sweet spot — low enough to care about improvement, high enough to have obvious mental game issues)
- At least 10 golfers in competitive settings (tournaments, club championships)

Best sourcing: your existing network, golf clubs, Reddit (r/golf), GolfWRX forums, local leagues.

### Beta structure

**Week 1:** Onboarding call (group Zoom or recorded video). Walk them through Master Quiz, first round flow, what to pay attention to.

**Weeks 2-5:** Play and log. At least 4 rounds during beta.

**Week 6:** Feedback survey + optional interviews with top users.

**What you're looking for:**
- Did Pattern Intelligence surprise them?
- What made them open the app between rounds?
- What almost made them quit?
- Would they pay $14.99/month?
- What's missing?

### Beta users become founders

Every beta user gets:
- Pro account free for 3 months post-launch
- Their name in the "Founding Members" section of the app (Account page)
- First access to the Pro Welcome Kit at cost
- A direct line to you for feedback

This creates evangelists. People who feel ownership tell everyone.

## THE COMPETITIVE LANDSCAPE

You have no direct competitor. This is important to understand and defend.

- **Calm / Headspace:** Generic mindfulness. Not golf-specific. No in-round presence.
- **Golf GPS apps (Arccos, Shot Scope):** Hardware-dependent. Track physical performance, not mental.
- **18Birdies / Golfshot:** Course management. Zero mental game.
- **Bob Rotella books / YouTube:** Passive consumption. No feedback loop.
- **Sports psychologists:** $200–$500/hour, not accessible, not in-round.

The Caddie owns a category. Not "golf app." Not "mindfulness app." **Mental game coaching, live, during play, that learns you.**

The moat is data + personalization. After 10 rounds, the app knows more about your mental game than any human coach you've ever worked with. That's the lock-in. That's why people don't leave.

## WHAT "GODDAMN THIS APP IS GONNA CHANGE MY GOLF GAME" ACTUALLY FEELS LIKE

It's not any single feature. It's a series of moments:

1. **Round 1, hole 3:** The coach references your biggest mental challenge from the quiz. "You said first-tee nerves are your thing — how are you feeling right now?" You didn't expect that. That's the hook.

2. **Round 2, 20-minute check-in on the back 9:** The app asks "what's the one thought you keep coming back to?" You type it. The coach reframes it in one line. You play the next hole differently.

3. **Round 4, end-of-round reveal:** Mental Handicap: 58. It was 39 on round 1. That number moved. You made it move. That's tangible progress.

4. **Round 7, Pattern Intelligence fires:** "In your last 5 rounds, your lowest emotional state consistently occurs holes 14-16." You thought about this after your last round. You just didn't have the data. Now you do.

5. **Round 10:** You're on 16, one over, playing the best round of your year. You open the app. The check-in fires. The coach says your cue word. You breathe. You birdie 17. You par 18. Career best.

**You share the round card. It says "Golf is 90% mental. The Caddie is the other 90%."**

Your playing partners ask what you used.

That's the word-of-mouth engine. That's how this becomes a brand, not just an app.
